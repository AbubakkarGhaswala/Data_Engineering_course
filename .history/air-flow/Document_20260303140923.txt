so Apache Airflow is an open-source platform used to programmatically author, schedule, and monitor batch-oriented workflows and data pipelines. Written in Python, it allows developers to create, visualize, and manage complex, dynamic workflows as Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs). Airflow is widely used for ETL/ELT tasks and integrates with cloud services like AWS and GCP.
