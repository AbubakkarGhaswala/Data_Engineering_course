A role is not recreated for each project. You create a role once and reuse it across multiple jobs or projects that need the same permissions. You don't create a new role every time.
Also add one more important point — why Role and not User for Glue? Because Glue is a service, not a human. Services cannot have usernames and passwords. So AWS gives them temporary credentials through a Role every time they run a job.
